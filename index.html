Pi Drive Backup

A personal backup tool. It is not a public service.

Pi Drive Backup is a private setup of rclone that runs on one home Raspberry Pi. It copies that Pi's backup files to its owner's own Google Drive and reads them back when a restore is needed.

Who can use it

Only the owner, @nutdevza, signs in to it. It is not offered to anyone else, and no one else's account is connected.

What it does with Google Drive

It uploads, lists, downloads and deletes backup files in the owner's Drive. It does not read or change other files for any other purpose.

Privacy policy

Maintained by @nutdevza · Built on rclone, which is open source.
